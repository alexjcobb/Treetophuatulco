The villa

Designed for calm, privacy, and connection

Four distinct suites, generous terraces, and open-air living spaces make TreeTop Villa equally suited to families, couples, and longer restorative stays.

Layout

Private top-floor primary suite + main-floor social living

The top-floor primary suite enjoys full privacy and direct access to the full-length deck and treetop island seating. All other bedrooms sit on the main floor with convenient access to terraces, the pool area, and shared living spaces.

Primary suite King bed, ensuite with double vanity, indoor shower + private outdoor shower, A/C, office with dual-monitor hookups, mini fridge, coffee maker, and direct access to treetop deck.
Bedroom 2 King bed, ensuite shower bath, private terrace access, A/C.
Bedroom 3 King bed, private terrace access, shared full bath with media room, A/C.
Media room / Bedroom 4 L-shaped sectional converts into two XL twin beds, shared full bath, A/C.
Common areas Open-air living room, modern kitchen, dining room table for eight, powder room, Sonos throughout, smart TVs in all suites.
Indoor amenities

High-comfort essentials

Living & dining rooms

Open-air living room with sweeping ocean breezes, dining for eight, and direct flow to the pool deck and bedroom suites.

Kitchen

Fully equipped modern kitchen for everyday cooking and easy entertaining.

Smart home convenience

Lighting, climate, music, and entertainment are managed through a single iPad.

Filtered water throughout

A whole-home filtered water system extends from all faucets to all showers for added comfort and peace of mind.
