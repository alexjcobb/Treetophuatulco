Location

All Huatulco offers is at your doorstep

TreeTop Villa sits just above the shoreline with direct, easy access to a calm private cove and a safe swimming beach—while still being minutes from resort dining, shopping, and town.

Why guests love the setting

Private enough to feel hidden, close enough to feel easy

The villa offers a rare blend of seclusion and convenience. Within the gated community, days can unfold between the beach, cove, terraces, pool deck, and neighborhood walks. When you want to venture out, Huatulco’s cafés, restaurants, and shops are nearby.

  • Protected waters ideal for swimming, floating, and paddleboarding
  • Quick access to the resort district without sacrificing quiet
  • Easy arrival and departure with the airport only 20 minutes away
